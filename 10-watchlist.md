- Tier A = check weekly. Tier B = check biweekly. Tier C = monthly.
- When a role opens that fits, move it to "Active applications" in `04-search-log.md`.

## How to use this watchlist

### Automated weekly check (cron job)
- Runs every Monday at 8am
- Checks Tier A career pages for new roles matching keywords: "creative operations", "content operations", "creative production", "studio operations", "head of operations", "VP operations", "COO", "content supply chain"
- Delivers a summary to this chat with any new matches + status changes
- You don't need to remember — it comes to you

### Manual touch (when you have time)
- Open this file before any outreach session
- Check "Status" column — update with anything you learn (interviews, rejections, new contacts)
- Move active applications to `04-search-log.md`
- Add new companies you discover to the right tier

### What counts as "key news"
- New funding round (means they'll be hiring)
- Senior leadership departure (means a role may open)
- Product launch in AI/content (means the org is growing in your direction)
- Acquisition or expansion (means new roles)
- The cron job will surface these in its weekly summary
