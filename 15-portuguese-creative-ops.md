## TIER A — Strongest fits (Porto-region + real creative/content production at scale)

### 1. Prozis
- **Location:** Maia (Porto metro area) — also Póvoa de Lanhoso, Braga, Esposende
- **Description:** Portuguese D2C powerhouse (sports nutrition, supplements, activewear, food) that does everything in-house: "idea, concept, design, manufacturing, quality control, marketing, sales, logistics, printing, distribution, client support, software development, photo, video, 3D."
- **Why it fits Rui:** Careers page explicitly lists in-house "photo, video, 3D" content production as a core function — exactly Rui's wheelhouse (creative content at scale for e-commerce, owned brands, product photography). Vertically integrated D2C model mirrors Farfetch's content supply chain demands. Active hiring (23 open positions as of research date).
- **Careers URL:** https://careers.prozis.com/en
- **Stage/size:** ~1,000 employees, ~€190M revenue (2023), founded 2007. Privately held, profitable, growing.

### 2. Sonae MC (Continente, Note, Bagga)
- **Location:** Maia (Porto) — Sonae headquarters
- **Description:** Portugal's largest food retailer; operates Continente hypermarkets/supermarkets, Note, and multiple private-label brands. Confirmed in-house "Digital & Content Marketing Director" role leading content strategy, social media, and influencer marketing for Continente.
- **Why it fits Rui:** Massive private-label portfolio (Continente brand across food, FMCG, lifestyle) requiring continuous product photography, packaging content, brand campaigns, and digital content. The existing "Digital & Content Marketing Director" function signals an established content ops function Rui could lead/elevate. Porto-based HQ.
- **Careers URL:** https://www.sonae.pt/en/people/opportunities-of-career/
- **Stage/size:** Sonae Group ~50,000+ employees; Sonae MC is the largest business unit, multi-billion euro revenue. Continente has 500+ stores.

### 3. Worten (Sonae SR)
- **Location:** Maia (Porto) — part of Sonae Group
- **Description:** Portugal's leading consumer electronics retailer (~35% market share, 200+ stores) operating the country's most-visited marketplace (worten.pt, 6M+ monthly visits) with 3,000+ sellers.
- **Why it fits Rui:** Marketplace + retail media model means high-volume content production: product photography, seller content, brand campaigns, retail media creative. Public references to "Retail Media" growth (300% revenue growth) and "commercial-grade creatives: studio-quality 360° photography, infographics, A+ content." Marketplace content supply chain is directly transferable from Farfetch.
- **Careers URL:** https://www.worten.pt/ (careers via Sonae portal)
- **Stage/size:** ~3,800 employees, marketplace leader in Portugal. Part of Sonae SR.

### 4. Sonae SR — MO / Sport Zone / Zippy (fashion & sports brands)
- **Location:** Maia (Porto) — Sonae headquarters
- **Description:** Sonae's Sports & Fashion division operating MO (fashion), Sport Zone (sports retail), Zippy (kids fashion), Losan, and Dott — 500+ stores across multiple countries.
- **Why it fits Rui:** Multi-brand fashion portfolio requiring e-commerce content production (product photography, lookbooks, campaigns, seasonal content) across MO, Zippy, and Dott. Direct parallel to Farfetch's multi-brand content model. Porto HQ. Sonae SR also owns Salsa Jeans (already on list), but MO/Zippy/Sport Zone are separate content operations.
- **Careers URL:** https://www.sonae.pt/en/people/opportunities-of-career/
- **Stage/size:** 500+ stores, Sonae SR turnover ~€2B+. Multi-country operations.
