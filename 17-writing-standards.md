# Writing Standards (applied to every draft before sending to Rui)

These gate every user-facing draft produced by the coaching workflow: LinkedIn
headline/About/opener, narrative variants, warm-contact outreach, interview
answers, and cockpit copy. Load the `humanizer` skill and enforce all of the
below before anything reaches Rui.

1. **HUMANIZER PASS** — run `humanizer` on the draft. Strip all 34 documented
   AI patterns (em-dash overuse, rule-of-three, copula avoidance, fake
   significance, sycophantic tone, etc.). Preserve Rui's own supplied wording
   **verbatim** — his phrasing is better than the corporate rewrite.

2. **NO NUMBERS in LinkedIn headline or About opener** — 12-senior-peer
   benchmark (July 2026). Headlines = title + company (+ optional short
   tagline). Numbers live in Experience bullets.

3. **NO fake compliments, NO assumed familiarity** in outreach — Rui's
   explicit rule. State the real connection, state the situation, make a
   specific low-stakes ask. Do not invent "I've been following your company."

4. **Side project = "side venture, labor of love, when time allows"** — never
   "sabbatical", never "runs in parallel" (reads as competing with the job),
   never "failed venture". Mirror Rui's own framing exactly.

5. **Consistent numbers everywhere**: 600+ FTE (900+ with seasonal/temp
   staff), €40M+ P&L. When one number changes in the About, align every
   Experience bullet. Ask Rui which set is correct — do not guess.

6. **Present options, do not decide for Rui.** If anything reads as the coach
   making a choice for him (e.g. sector exclusion), flag it for pushback.

7. **About opens narratively**, not with a metric or a work-authorization
   line. End cleanly at the positioning statement. Open-to-Work / geo /
   authorization live in LinkedIn's dedicated fields, not the About.

8. **Open to Work = Recruiters only** (not Public) for a senior candidate with
   a gap. Director-level for all 5 titles when urgency is high; the title is
   negotiable upward once a company wants him.
