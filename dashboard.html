Network activation designed for a Relator who hates networking

The reframe: This is NOT networking. It's "market intelligence conversations" and "reconnecting with former colleagues." Never use the word "networking" in your planning. The research is clear: the label determines whether you engage or avoid.

The methodology

Dormant Tie Reactivation Protocol — synthesised from 3 peer-reviewed studies (Levin 2011, Rondi 2023, Yang 2021).

Evidence: reconnecting dormant ties produces outcomes comparable to active ties. Previously STRONG dormant ties give BOTH trust AND novel information — the best of both worlds. People under job insecurity naturally reactivate dormant ties (it's a researched coping mechanism, not desperation).

Why it fits you

You're not "bad at networking" — you're bad at the extrovert version. This approach works WITH your wiring:

Relator #1 → deep not wide; these are people who already know you
Deliberative #2 → structured protocol, not open-ended anxiety
Learner #3 → framed as "research conversations"
Analytical #4 → track outcomes, analyse yield

The protocol — 5 steps

Step 1 · Map & tier (one-time, 1 hr)

Export LinkedIn connections (Settings → Data Privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections). Segment into: direct reports, cross-functional peers, vendors/agencies, industry contacts. Tier each: A (would definitely take your call), B (would probably respond). Only A+B go on active list (~130-200 people). Don't map all 600.

Step 2 · Reactivation message

Hi [Name], it's been a while — I think we last connected at [specific shared context]. I hope you're doing well. I saw [specific recent thing about them] and wanted to reach out. I'm currently exploring senior creative ops roles and would really value your perspective on [specific thing they'd know about]. Would you have 20 minutes for a quick call? No pressure — I'd just love to catch up and hear what you're working on.

Acknowledge the gap · catch up first · low-stakes ask · reference something specific · frame as exploration

Step 3 · The conversation (TIARA)

Structured 20-min call. Listen 80%, talk 20%. Use TIARA questions:

Trends — what's changing in your field?
Insights — why does this work interest you?
Advice — how did you get your role?
Resources — who else should I talk to? ← referrals happen here
Assignments — what should I learn?

NEVER ask for a job. Ask for information + referrals. The relationship does the work.

Step 4 · Follow-up

Within 24 hrs: thank-you referencing something specific they said. If referred: contact the referral, name-drop ("[Name] suggested I reach out"). Follow up again in 4-6 weeks with something relevant.

Step 5 · Cadence

2-3 reactivation messages per week. Not 50. Start with 5 Tier-A contacts. Do NOT proceed to Tier B until Tier A is exhausted. Expect 30-50% response rate.

The warm-up (optional but powerful)

5-Minute Favor (Adam Rifkin / Adam Grant): before the reactivation message, send a small favor — an article, an introduction, a LinkedIn endorsement. "Saw this and thought of you — no response needed." Builds social capital; makes the later ask feel natural. Ideal for a Relator — offering help is less aversive than asking.
