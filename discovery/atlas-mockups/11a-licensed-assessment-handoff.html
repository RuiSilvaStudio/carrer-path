The official interest exercise

To preserve the validated tool unchanged, Atlas presents the official O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form as a distinct assessment sheet. Atlas does not rewrite, translate, shorten, or reinterpret the questions.

Which activities would you enjoy?

The real production sheet would contain the complete official questionnaire, response options, and score method exactly as published. This mockup deliberately does not reproduce the questions.

Official item and response content remains fixed. Atlas adds no wording, no hidden weighting, and no personalised conclusion inside the instrument.
What happens after it: Atlas records the six interest scores you choose to save, then compares them separately with role work-environment references, your demonstrated evidence, constraints, and market context.

Your directions remain available whether you complete this or not.

What stays clear

Atlas is the guide.

It explains why an assessment may help, preserves user control, and connects results to the wider decision.

The instrument stays intact.

Its questions, answer scale, scoring, and official explanation are not redesigned to fit the app.

Portugal/EU context stays separate.

O*NET supplies a US interest reference only. It does not define local demand, pay, eligibility, or likely outcomes.
