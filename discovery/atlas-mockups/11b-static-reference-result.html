How Atlas will use this—carefully

01

Compare work environments, not people.

For a confirmed role reference, Atlas may compare your six interests with a versioned occupational interest profile from downloaded O*NET data. It shows resemblance by dimension, never a single career score.

02

Keep the source boundary visible.

O*NET occupational profiles are US reference data. Atlas uses ESCO, ISCO, and CPP-2010 for the European/Portugal role backbone; it does not turn this reference into a Portugal hiring forecast.
