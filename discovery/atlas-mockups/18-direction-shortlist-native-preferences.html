Keep the logic visible

Preferences are not a verdict

They are user-controlled statements. They do not prove competence, eligibility, or likely satisfaction.

Titles are hypotheses

Atlas retains the title you use and asks for context before treating a role reference as confirmed.

Evidence can contradict the suggestion

You can remove a direction, add counter-evidence, or keep it only as an idea to test.

Why did a direction appear?
Atlas exposes the inputs it used. It does not claim to have discovered your “true career.”
No personality label, overall fit score, individual hiring prediction, or unverified Portugal demand claim determined these directions. Optional O*NET Database reference does not generate the list and is never market evidence.
