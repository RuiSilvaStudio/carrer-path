# Atlas — Current recommended discovery flow

**Status:** approved product direction after the native-preferences decision — 30 July 2026

These screens show the current recommended experience. They replace the earlier O*NET/RIASEC-first exploration as the product path.

## The user journey

1. **Career profile** — `03-career-profile-entry.html`  
   The user adds their own career history, current reality, practical constraints, and initial direction ideas.

2. **Reflective work preferences** — `12-work-preferences.html`  
   A short form the user fills in about contribution style, working environment, and practical conditions. It is editable, skippable, and explicitly not a psychological assessment.

3. **Preference brief** — `13-preferences-handoff.html`  
   Atlas reflects the user’s stated preferences back in plain language and shows that each may be muted or edited before it affects a comparison.

4. **Direction shortlist** — `18-direction-shortlist-native-preferences.html`  
   The user keeps, removes, adds, and selects directions to compare. Each direction exposes why it appeared: stated preferences, demonstrated evidence, and the quality of the role reference—never a hidden score or RIASEC code.

5. **Evidence-led role comparison** — `14-role-comparison-native-preferences.html`  
   A small number of user-chosen directions are compared across separate lenses: stated preferences, demonstrated evidence, role/work-context reference, Portugal/EU market context, and one useful next test. No overall fit score.

6. **Direction brief** — `15-direction-detail-native-preferences.html`  
   The user examines one direction in depth: what they said matters, evidence that supports it, gaps/unknowns, bounded role reference, and the smallest credible test.

7. **Direction workspace** — `09-direction-workspace.html`  
   A light 30–60 day evidence cycle. It is not a task board: actions exist only to test material assumptions.

8. **Market Pulse** — `16-market-pulse-evidence-first.html`  
   Dated Portugal-first market evidence that changes—or does not change—the next test. It is not career news or a personal forecast.

9. **Reassessment** — `17-reassess-direction.html`  
   The user decides whether to continue, adjust, pause, or deprioritise a direction. The rationale, evidence, and uncertainty remain available; no direction is silently deleted.

## What stays separate across every screen

- **Stated preferences** — user-controlled and editable; not personality or ability.
- **Demonstrated evidence** — what the user has actually done or can show.
- **Role reference** — ESCO/ISCO/CPP is the EU/Portugal backbone.
- **Optional O*NET Database reference** — versioned US work-context/task reference only through a documented crosswalk; never a market claim or user assessment.
- **Market evidence** — source-cited, dated, geographic, and scoped; not an individual forecast.
- **Unknowns** — visible rather than replaced with artificial confidence.
