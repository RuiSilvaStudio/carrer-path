The problem

Most career tools are a racket.

You already suspect it. A five-minute quiz declares you a "Visionary Architect," then a paywall appears before anything useful does. You're left with a label, not a direction.

/ 01

The quiz tells you what you already said

Most assessments just mirror your answers back with a flattering label. No outside evidence, no labor market, no test of whether the suggestion survives contact with reality.

/ 02

The upsell starts before the value does

The "free report" is a teaser. The real reading, the real careers, the real plan — each sits behind the next payment. You're being sold certainty, one drip at a time.

/ 03

Your data becomes the product

Your answers about hopes, fears, and work history are valuable. Many tools monetize exactly that. You get a horoscope; they get a profile they can sell.
