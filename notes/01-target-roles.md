# Target roles — where Rui fits tight

Center of gravity: **senior Creative Operations / Creative Production leadership — leading teams + process at scale.** Everything below is a variation on that.

## A. Primary job titles to search (highest fit)

- VP / SVP / Head of Creative Operations
- VP / Director of Creative Production
- Director / Head of Content Operations
- Director of Production (creative/content/photo studio)
- Head of Studio Operations
- Director of Brand Operations
- Director of Creative Services (internal creative org)
- Head of Digital Asset Management / Content Supply Chain

## B. Adjacent titles (strong fit, wider pool)

- Director of Operations (creative/content/e-commerce org)
- Director of E-commerce Operations
- Head of Content (operations-flavored, not editorial)
- Director of Workflow / Process Excellence (creative context)
- Director of Photo Studio Operations
- Director of Product Content
- Head of Localization / Translation Operations
- Director of AI / Automation (creative content context)

## C. Industry archetypes (in order of fit)

1. **Luxury fashion / multi-brand e-commerce** (native): FARFETCH, Net-a-Porter, Mytheresa, SSENSE, Matches, Yoox Net-a-Porter, Luisaviaroma, Moda Operandi, Olympia.
2. **Marketplaces with large content orgs**: Etsy, eBay, Vinted, Wallapop, Bol, Allegro, Zalando, About You, ASOS.
3. **D2C / consumer brands with heavy content needs**: Nike, Adidas, Zara/Inditex, Mango, H&M group, Sonos, Apple (creative services), Bose.
4. **Tech companies with creative orgs**: Shopify, Meta (content ops), Amazon (fashion/content), Google (creative lab ops).
5. **Creative agencies / brand studios** (client-facing): Wieden+Kennedy, Droga5, Mother, AKQA, R/GA, Iris.
6. **Furniture / design / home brands** (stretch but credible): Herman Miller/MillerKnoll, Vitra, USM, Aram, Made.com, Swoon, Westwing, Chairish, 1stDibs.

## D. Geographic filter

- **Remote-anywhere** (global, English-speaking): prioritize — widens pool dramatically. Many of the above hire EU remote.
- **Portugal / Porto onsite or hybrid**: Lisbon/Porto tech hub has Farfetch-adjacent roles (OutSystems, Talkdesk, Anchor, etc.) but creative-ops-specific roles are rarer; pair with remote search.
- **EU hubs with strong creative-ops markets**: London, Amsterdam, Berlin, Paris, Milan. Rui's PT/EU work eligibility is an asset.

## F. Red flags — roles to skip (waste of time)

- Pure individual-contributor design roles (no team leadership).
- "Creative Director" with no operations/production remit (wrong profile — Rui leads the org, not the campaign aesthetic).
- Early-stage startups with no existing creative org to lead (he'd be building from zero, not leading at scale).
- Agencies where the role is purely account/client management (not ops).
- Anything requiring <5 years experience (signals junior org).
