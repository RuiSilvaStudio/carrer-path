## 6. Development areas (honest, for interviews) → pre-empted

These are the areas that will come up in senior interviews. Better to address them proactively than get caught.

1. **Perfectionism / over-driving** — "My drive for quality and precision is my biggest strength, and I've learned to watch it. Earlier in my career I could over-control under pressure — I've gotten better at distinguishing where precision matters (luxury quality, SLAs) and where 'good and done' beats 'perfect and late.'"
   - *Source: LCP Driven #1 reactive + Perfect #2 + DiSC High C stress pattern + Deliberative #2.*

2. **Under-claiming strengths** — "360° feedback showed me that others consistently rated me higher than I rated myself. I'm Deliberative — I tend to caveat and qualify. I've learned that in interviews and stakeholder settings, under-claiming doesn't read as humility; it reads as uncertainty. I'm working on claiming my work plainly."
   - *Source: LCP 360° gap. This is directly actionable in interview prep — practice claiming achievements without hedging.*

3. **Networking breadth** — "I'm a Relator — I go deep with a small network rather than wide. It's why I stayed 15 years at one company. I'm now deliberately expanding my network for the first time, which is uncomfortable but necessary."
   - *Source: Relator #1 + no active referrals + 15-year single-company tenure.*

4. **Task-relationship balance** — "My LCP showed a task-dominant balance. I care deeply about people (it's my top creative competency), but in practice I lead with the work first. In cultures that need more relational warmth upfront, I've learned to invest in the relationship before jumping to the task."
   - *Source: LCP Relationship-Task Balance ~40 + Empathy #29 + Positivity #19.*

5. **Reactive decisiveness under pressure** — "Under pressure I can become autocratic — deciding fast and directing hard. It gets results in crises but can shut down input. I've learned to pause and ask 'who has information I don't?' before deciding, even when the clock is ticking."
   - *Source: LCP Autocratic + Controlling + Decisiveness (all elevated reactive).*

## 7. Interview calibration (how to show up)

Based on the profile, here's how Rui should calibrate in interviews:

- **Lead with competence and numbers, not warmth.** High C + Deliberative + low Positivity = warmth isn't the natural register. That's fine — senior ops roles screen for competence. Open with quantified achievements, not relationship-building stories.
- **But have 2 relational stories ready.** The LCP shows Caring Connection and Selfless Leader as top creative competencies — *others* see this in Rui even if it's not his default register. Have 2 stories where you built a team, developed someone, or led through trust. (The 900-person org, building LA/HK teams, promoting people.)
- **Claim achievements without hedging.** The 360° gap is the biggest interview risk — Rui will naturally caveat ("it was a team effort," "we were lucky with timing"). Practice saying "I drove X" plainly.
- **Address perfectionism proactively** if asked about weaknesses — it's the most authentic and most defensible answer, backed by data.
- **In outreach messages:** lead with data (High C), use the i secondary for warmth in the opening/closing. Don't over-effuse — Relator + Deliberative means authentic, specific, brief beats enthusiastic-generic.
