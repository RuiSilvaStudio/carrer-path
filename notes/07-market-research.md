## 3. Market reality — 2025-2026 senior hiring trends

### The hard truth

| Dimension | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Market direction** | **Shrinking.** U.S. hiring down 8.7% YoY. Only 181K jobs added in all of 2025 vs. 1.46M in 2024. Senior creative ops not on fastest-growing lists. ~443 "Director Creative Operations" roles on LinkedIn globally — small absolute number. | LinkedIn Economic Graph, Oct 2025 |
| **Who's hiring** | AI/tech creative studios, gaming/interactive, healthcare/pharma marketing, fintech | Multiple |
| **Who's pulled back hardest** | **Luxury fashion/e-commerce (your sector), pure tech/SaaS, traditional ad agencies** | Multiple |
| **What changed in screening** | AI/automation fluency now table-stakes. Revenue attribution expected (not just cost). Team-reduction experience valued if framed as capability. Hybrid/onsite returning for senior ("FOBO" — fear of better options). Skills-based screening replacing credential-based. | Robert Half 2026, SHRM |
| **Long-term unemployment penalty** | **Severe.** Callback rates drop from 11% (no gap) to 4-5% (2-3yr gap). Penalty turns negative after 6 months and compounds. 1 in 4 U.S. unemployed now searching 6+ months. White-collar hit hardest. Age compounds the penalty. | Ghent University 2024 meta-analysis, CNBC Feb 2026, WSJ, AARP, Forbes |
| **EU remote senior ops** | Real but rare. ~67 on Glassdoor, ~28 on RemoteRocketship, ~74 on LinkedIn for all Europe. Netherlands/Germany/UK have most; Portugal ~37 Creative Director roles (many hybrid Lisbon/Porto). Trend shifting back to hybrid. | Glassdoor, RemoteRocketship, Hays, Robert Half |
| **Hiring cycles** | **Nearly doubled.** Time-to-fill: 36 days (2024) → 42 (2025) → 63-68 days (Jan 2026). Director: ~90 days. Executive: ~120 days. ~40% of senior roles take >90 days. Some candidates reporting 8 interview rounds. Top candidates off market in ~10 days. | Multiple |

### What this means for you, bluntly

1. **Your background maps to the worst-hit hiring sector** (luxury fashion/e-commerce). This is the single biggest headwind.
2. **Your 16-month gap puts you past the callback-penalty inflection point** (6 months). Cold applications will be filtered.
3. **Cold applications are very low-odds in this market.** Referrals are the highest-leverage path.
4. **Expect 3-4 month hiring cycles.** Run 5-10 parallel processes — don't go sequential.
5. **AI/automation fluency is now table-stakes** — but it's also your differentiator. You've *deployed* AI for content automation (80% categorization, ML translation). Most candidates haven't. Lead with it.

### The mitigation playbook

1. **Pivot target industries** to AI/gaming/pharma/streaming (where hiring is active), away from luxury fashion (where it's frozen).
2. **Build referral pipeline** — this is the single highest-leverage action. Cold applications hit the gap penalty hardest.
3. **Demonstrate AI/automation fluency publicly** to counter the "skills decay" assumption. A short post, a case study, anything visible.
4. **Run many parallel processes** given 90-120 day cycles.
5. **Reposition as "Content Supply Chain"** not "Creative Operations" — this is the umbrella term that surfaces in searches across all 7 hiring industries.
