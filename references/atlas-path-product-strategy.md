# Atlas Path — Product Strategy Notes

## Brand
- Live product name: "The Atlas Path"
- Domain: `atlaspath.eu`
- Rebranded from "The Personality Atlas" on 2026-08-02

## Positioning
- Free-first, transparent pricing, no upsell ladder.
- Explicitly anti-pattern: scammy assessment tools that hide outcomes, drip-sell, and extract via opacity.

## Monetization hypothesis
- First paid trigger: quantified skill proximity + live demand signal.
- Retention layer: transition experiment designer.
- B2B bridge: shareable professional brief export.
- Full proposal: `atlas-path-business-model-proposal.md`

## Anti-features
Do not build: personality tests, generic AI chat, coaching marketplace, job board aggregator, resume builder.
