# Startup Opportunities for Senior Creative Operations Leader
## Research for Rui Silva (ex-SVP Creative Operations, FARFETCH)

### Profile fit
- 15 years scaling creative engine (Prod Mgr → SVP), 900+ team, 5 countries
- €50M+ P&L, cut op spend 10%→1% of sales
- AI/ML automation, Lean/Kaizen, Virtual Model tech (€60→€30), 3.6M assets/yr, ML translation 12 langs
- CSPO certified, furniture studio side venture (can build from zero)
- Based in Guimarães Portugal, open to remote-anywhere
- Heavy on scale, process engineering, AI/ML for content automation, cross-cultural team leadership
- NOT a pure designer — an ops leader with craft/design sensibility

### Key findings summary
1. **Best-fit stage**: Series B and growth (Series C+). Series A is borderline — only if the role is "founding ops lead" not "senior creative ops leader." Seed is too early.
2. **Best-fit sectors**: AI-content/video startups, creator economy platforms, D2C brands, e-commerce infrastructure/marketplaces. Fintech and healthtech need content ops but the role is narrower.
3. **Titles**: Head of Operations, COO, VP Operations, Head of Creative Ops, Head of Content Operations, Founding Ops Lead.
4. **Comp**: Europe €120-180k cash + 0.5-2% equity at Series A/B. US $170-300k cash + 0.1-1.5% equity. Operations equity grants have shrunk 50% since 2022 (Carta).
5. **Risk-reward**: Gain = equity upside, scope, speed, AI-native operating model. Lose = cash haircut, structure, benefits, job security.
6. **10-15 specific startups**: See startups-list.md
7. **How to find roles**: Mostly NOT on LinkedIn. Use VC talent partner networks, founder direct outreach, Ashby job boards, Sifted leaderboards, curated talent scouts.
