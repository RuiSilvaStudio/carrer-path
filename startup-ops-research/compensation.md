## Carta H1 2025 macro trends (critical context)
- **Operations equity grants have shrunk ~50% since late 2022.** The average initial equity grant for ops roles is half what it was 3 years ago.
- Operations as a % of new hires is declining — engineering (29.7%) and sales (16.6%) dominate new hires. Ops is being lean-staffed.
- Average startup salary up 5.8% since April 2022, but gains are concentrated in legal (+10%), product, design, marketing, and AI/ML engineering.
- Hardware and medical devices are the only sectors with net positive headcount growth (1.3 and 1.2 hires per departure).
- Consumer, pharma/biotech, gaming, and education are shrinking headcount.

## Key takeaways for Rui

1. **Europe pays ~20-30% less in cash than US for the same role, but equity is often higher and benefits are stronger.** Net lifestyle may be comparable or better in Europe on after-tax basis.

2. **At Series B (the sweet spot for Rui), expect €150-200k cash + 0.5-1.5% equity in Europe.** US equivalent: $170-250k + 0.2-1.0%.

3. **Operations equity is compressed.** The 1% median from the 2022 Sifted survey is now closer to 0.3-0.8% for new hires at Series B/C, per Carta's 50% shrinkage finding. Negotiate hard on equity — it's the only real upside.

4. **AI-content startups may pay a premium.** Carta shows AI/ML roles gained 5-9% salary growth. If Rui positions as "AI content automation leader" rather than generic ops, he can command the top of the band.

5. **The "founding ops" premium is real.** Joining at Series A as the first ops hire (not at Series B as employee #50) roughly doubles equity (1.5-2.5% vs 0.5-1%) at the cost of 20-30% lower cash and higher risk.

6. **At Rui's seniority, target the top 25th percentile, not median.** His SVP/€50M P&L/900-team profile is top 5% of ops candidates. He should not accept median offers. Ask for: €180-220k cash + 1-2% equity at Series B AI-content or D2C startups.
