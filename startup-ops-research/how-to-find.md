# How to Find Startup Senior Ops Roles (Most Are NOT on LinkedIn)

## The core problem
Most senior startup ops hires are NOT posted on LinkedIn job boards. They are filled through:
1. Founder/VC networks (referrals)
2. Executive search / talent partners retained by VCs
3. Company career pages (often on Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever — not LinkedIn)
4. Direct founder outreach
5. Niche startup job boards

## Channels to use (ranked by priority for Rui)

### 1. Direct founder outreach (HIGHEST YIELD)
- **How:** Identify 20-30 target startups (use the Sifted 250 list and sector-specific research). Find founders on LinkedIn. Send a short, specific message: "I built the Virtual Model + ML translation engine at FARFETCH (3.6M assets/yr, 80% automation). I see [company] is scaling content production — would love to talk about how I could help build your ops engine."
- **Why it works:** Founders at Series A/B companies are always looking for senior ops leaders but rarely post the role publicly. They hire from their network. If you reach out with a specific value prop, you become their network.
- **Response rate:** Expect 10-20% response rate from cold outreach if the message is specific and the startup is a fit. That's 2-6 conversations from 30 messages.
- **Template:** See outreach-template.md

### 2. VC talent partners / portfolio talent teams
- **How:** Most major European VC firms have a "talent partner" or "portfolio talent" team whose job is to help their portfolio companies hire. Reach out to these people directly. They have a roster of portfolio companies actively hiring ops leaders.
- **Target VCs (Europe):**
  - **Balderton Capital** — talent team works with portfolio (Vinted, Yazen-type companies)
  - **Index Ventures** — portfolio includes many consumer/content startups
  - **Accel London** — large portfolio, active talent team
  - **Northzone** — portfolio includes Klarna-type companies
  - **EQT Ventures** — strong on AI/content startups
  - **Earlybird** — German/European focus, AI portfolio
  - **Seedcamp** — early stage, but their alumni network is huge
  - **LocalGlobe** — UK-focused, consumer/content portfolio
- **How to reach them:** Search "talent partner [VC name]" on LinkedIn. Or go to the VC's website and find the "portfolio" page — then approach the talent team about a specific portfolio company.
- **Why it works:** VC talent partners are incentivized to place senior leaders into their portfolio companies. They often know about unposted roles.

### 3. Company career pages (on ATS platforms, not LinkedIn)
- **Ashby (ashbyhq.com):** Many well-funded startups use Ashby for their careers page. Search: site:jobs.ashbyhq.com "operations" OR "creative" OR "content"
- **Greenhouse (greenhouse.io):** Larger startups use Greenhouse. Search their boards directly.
- **Lever (lever.co):** Mid-stage startups use Lever.
- **Why this matters:** These ATS career pages often have roles that are never cross-posted to LinkedIn. Synthesia's "Creative Operations Leader" role was on Ashby but not prominently on LinkedIn.

### 4. Niche startup job boards
- **startup.jobs** — actively lists Head of Operations roles (15+ open as of July 2026). Filter by "remote" and "Europe."
- **topstartups.io/jobs** — filter by role (Business Operations), stage (Series A/B/C), location (Remote/Europe). Shows which startups are funded by Sequoia, YC, A16Z.
- **Sifted Jobs** — sifted.eu/jobs. Europe-focused, curated.
- **Remote in Europe** — remoteineurope.com. Curated remote tech jobs in Europe.
- **Creator Economy Jobs** — creatoreconomyjobs.co. Niche for creator economy platforms.
- **Generalist.world/jobs** — "for roles too weird to post anywhere else." Good for unusual hybrid roles.
- **VC Stack job board** — vcstack.com/job. VC-portfolio roles.
- **EverythingMarketplaces.com** — marketplace-specific startup jobs.

### 5. LinkedIn — but used strategically, not passively
- **Don't:** Apply to LinkedIn job postings and wait. Most senior ops roles aren't there.
- **Do:** Search for "Head of Operations" OR "VP Operations" OR "Creative Operations" at companies that just raised Series B/C. Use Crunchbase or Sifted to find recently-funded companies, then search LinkedIn for their current ops leaders (to see if they're hiring) or message the CEO/founders directly.
- **Do:** Post content on LinkedIn that demonstrates Rui's expertise. "How we cut creative op spend from 10% to 1% at FARFETCH using ML translation and Virtual Models" — a post like this reaches founders who need exactly this. Make it a 5-part series.

### 6. Executive search / retained recruiters
- Some European exec search firms handle startup COO/VP Ops roles:
  - **Henley Search** — UK/Europe, tech and digital
  - **Grace Blue** — creative/digital leadership
  - **The Up Group** — European tech/digital leadership
  - **Anderson Frank** — tech-focused
- **Caveat:** These firms work on retained basis for companies, not candidates. But registering with them puts Rui in their database for when a mandate comes in.

### 7. Warm network reactivation
- Rui spent 15 years at FARFETCH. He has 1000+ connections from the creative/ops/fashion-tech world. Many of them are now at startups or have moved to startups.
- **Action:** Message 20 former FARFETCH colleagues who left for startups. Ask: "Where are you now, and is your team hiring senior ops?" This is the single highest-response channel.
- **Also:** FARFETCH alumni are likely at companies like LaLaLand, Heuritech, The Fabricant, and other fashion-tech startups. Check LinkedIn for "ex-FARFETCH" + current company.

### 8. Sifted Leaderboards as a prospecting tool
- The **Sifted 250** (sifted.eu/leaderboards/sifted-250-2025) lists Europe's 250 fastest-growing startups by revenue CAGR.
- The **Sifted 100** regional lists (UK, DACH, Southern Europe, Nordics) give 400 more companies.
- **How to use:** Download the list. Filter for companies in AI-content, D2C, marketplace, healthtech, edtech. Find their headcount (Crunchbase, LinkedIn company page). Target companies with 50-300 employees (Series B sweet spot). Reach out to founders.
