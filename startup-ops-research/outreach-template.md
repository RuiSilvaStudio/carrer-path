## Tips for outreach
1. **Personalize the first 2 sentences** — show you know the company. Don't use a generic template.
2. **Lead with numbers, not titles** — "3.6M assets/yr, €50M P&L, cut spend 10%→1%" is more compelling than "SVP Creative Operations."
3. **Make the parallel explicit** — connect what you did at FARFETCH to what they're building. Don't make them guess.
4. **Don't ask for a job directly** — ask for a conversation about their content ops challenges. Let the role emerge.
5. **Follow up once after 7-10 days** if no response. Don't follow up twice.
6. **Target founders/CEOs at Series A/B** — they're the decision-makers for senior ops hires. At Series C+ target the COO or Head of People.
