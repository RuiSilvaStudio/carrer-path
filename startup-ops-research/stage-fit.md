# Stage Fit — Which Startup Stages Need a Senior Creative Ops Leader

## Seed stage (~$1-5M raised, 1-10 employees)
**Verdict: TOO EARLY for a senior creative operations leader at Rui's level.**

- The role doesn't exist yet. Founders do everything themselves.
- A "Head of Operations" at seed is really a chief-of-staff / do-everything generalist, not a scaled-ops leader.
- Sifted data: seed COOs earn £96k median (€110k) — a ~70% pay cut from SVP-level corporate comp.
- The 1-10 person team can't absorb a 900-team, €50M-P&L operator. The gap in scope is too wide.
- Rui's skills (process engineering, ML automation at scale, multi-country team leadership) need scale to deploy.
- EXCEPTION: A seed-stage AI-content startup where the founder explicitly wants a "founding COO / founding ops leader" who can architect the operating model from zero — this fits Rui's "build from zero" furniture studio instinct. But equity must be founder-level (2-5%), not hire-level.

## Series A (~$5-15M raised, 10-50 employees)
**Verdict: BORDERLINE. Only if role is "Founding Ops Lead" or "Head of Operations (first ops hire)."**

- This is where the first dedicated operations leader typically comes in.
- SaaStr guidance: "At $1.5M ARR you should still be the COO" — true COO hires happen at $10M+ ARR.
- Series A companies hiring ops leaders are usually at $2-8M ARR, 15-40 people.
- Rui's profile is overpowered for most Series A roles — he'd be bored or frustrated by the lack of scale.
- BUT: Series A AI-content/video startups are the sweet spot. They have product-market fit, funding, and a burning need to build the content production engine. This is where Rui's Virtual Model + ML translation + creative automation experience is rare and valuable.
- Sifted data: Series A COO median £140k (€160k), equity 1-2% for non-founder.
- Titles to target: "Founding Operations Lead," "Head of Operations," "VP Operations."

## Series B (~$15-50M raised, 50-200 employees)
**Verdict: THE SWEET SPOT. This is where Rui's profile fits best.**

- Series B companies have product-market fit, $10-30M ARR, and are scaling headcount rapidly.
- This is exactly when "Head of Creative Operations" or "VP Content Operations" becomes a real, funded role.
- The pain point: a creative team of 5-15 people that was fine at Series A is now buckling under volume. Asset counts exploding. Localization needed. Process is broken. This is Rui's exact playbook.
- Series B companies have the cash to pay near-market salary AND meaningful equity.
- Sifted data: Series B COO median ~£150k (€170k), equity 0.5-1.5%.
- TopStartups.io: Head of Operations at Series A in Austin — $300k cash + 1.4% equity. At Series B, $170-200k + 0.5-1% is typical.
- Series B is also when companies start hiring specialized ops (content ops, creative ops, revenue ops) rather than a general COO — perfect for Rui's niche.

## Growth / Series C+ (~$50M+ raised, 200-1000 employees)
**Verdict: STRONG FIT, but equity is smaller and role may be narrower.**

- These companies have the scale that matches Rui's FARFETCH experience (200-1000 people, multi-country, real P&L).
- Cash comp is closest to corporate: $200-350k base for VP Operations / Head of Ops.
- BUT: equity is much smaller — 0.05-0.3% typical. The upside is lower, though risk is also lower.
- Carta H1 2025: operations as a % of new hires has been declining — companies favor engineering and sales hires. Operations equity grants are 50% smaller than in late 2022.
- The role here is often "Head of Content Operations" or "VP Creative Production" — a defined function, not a generalist COO. This suits Rui's specialization.
- Risk: joining a growth-stage startup is closer to a corporate role with equity — less of the "build from zero" upside but also less existential risk.

## Stage ranking for Rui
1. **Series B** — best fit. Scale to deploy skills, cash to pay, equity still meaningful.
2. **Growth/Series C+** — strong but narrower role, smaller equity.
3. **Series A (AI-content only)** — high upside, cash sacrifice, founding-type role.
4. **Seed** — only if "founding COO" with founder-level equity and Rui wants to build from zero.
