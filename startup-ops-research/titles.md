# Titles Startups Use for Creative/Content Operations Leadership

Startups use a scattered range of titles for this function. The title depends heavily on the company stage and sector. Here's what Rui should search for and target.

## Title categories

### 1. General operations leadership (most common at early-mid stage)
- **Head of Operations** — the most common title. Used at seed through Series B. Broad remit: operations, people, finance, content. This is what startup.jobs lists most actively (15+ open roles as of July 2026).
- **COO** — used at Series B+ when the company wants a clear #2. Sifted survey: COOs at seed earn £96k, Series A £140k, Series B £150k. Non-founder COO median equity 1%; founder COOs 5-24%.
- **VP Operations** — more common in US startups. TopStartups.io shows VP of Operations at Series B at $130-200k + 0.1-0.5% equity.
- **Founding Operations Lead / Founding COO** — used at seed/Series A to signal the person is building the ops function from zero. Equity is higher (1-2.5%).

### 2. Creative/content-specific operations (best fit for Rui's niche)
- **Head of Creative Operations** — the most direct match. Used by companies with a real creative production function. Synthesia is actively hiring this title (confirmed July 2026).
- **Head of Content Operations** — used by content-heavy platforms (media, creator economy, edtech). Vinted posted a "Creative Operations Manager" role with similar scope.
- **VP Creative Production** — used at growth-stage companies with large in-house creative teams (D2C, fashion, marketplaces).
- **Head of Content** — broader, includes content strategy + ops. Common at creator economy and media startups. Note: this role often skews editorial rather than operational. Rui should clarify scope.
- **Head of Production** — used in video/film/3D content startups. More craft-focused but ops-adjacent.
- **Director of Creative Services** — used at larger startups (Series C+) with internal agency model.

### 3. Adjacent titles worth searching
- **Head of Studio Operations** — video/3D content studios (Bambuser, LaLaLand, The Fabricant)
- **Head of Content Automation** / **Head of AI Content** — emerging title at AI-content startups. Very rare but high-fit.
- **Chief of Staff** — at seed/Series A, often the de facto ops leader. Lower equity than COO but real operational scope.
- **Head of Revenue Operations** — if the company sees creative/content as revenue-driving, this title appears. More common in US.
- **VP Business Operations** — generalist ops role at Series B+.

## What Rui should target
- **Primary:** Head of Creative Operations, Head of Content Operations, VP Creative Production
- **Secondary:** Head of Operations, VP Operations (at AI-content or D2C startups where ops = content ops)
- **Avoid:** Head of Content (too editorial), Chief of Staff (too junior), COO at seed (too generalist)

## Title red flags
- **"Operations Manager"** (not Head/VP) — usually a mid-level role, not senior leadership. Rui is overqualified.
- **"Content Manager"** — individual contributor or small team lead. Not a leadership role.
- **"Creative Director"** — this is a design/creative leadership role, not operations. Rui is not a designer.
- Any title at a seed-stage company with <10 employees — the scope will be too small regardless of title.

## Search terms for job boards
- "creative operations" + head/VP/director
- "content operations" + head/VP
- "head of operations" + creative/content/AI/video
- "VP operations" + startup/Series B
- "head of production" + content/digital/AI
- "founding operations" + creative/content
